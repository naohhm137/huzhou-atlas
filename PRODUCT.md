# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user. Vite, React, TypeScript, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Three.js were chosen to implement scroll controlled video and live 3D in one static site.

## Users

People curious about Huzhou who may not know its craft and tea traditions. Inferred from the approved cultural education brief.

## Product Purpose

Let visitors discover Huzhou through three craft objects, one architectural landmark, and four real city places, understanding both the objects' layers and the city's spatial scales through direct interaction.

## Positioning

One continuous story links scroll controlled film, manipulable 3D objects, and documented photographic city scenes. The film creates place and atmosphere; the 3D views explain each object; the later photo chapters move from street scale to skyline, leisure and water town.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Revised theme: “层见湖州”, a coherent atlas of brush, silk weaving, tea ware, and Feiying Pagoda. The user explicitly requested the tower and a broader theme.
- Include both effects demonstrated in the supplied tutorials: scroll scrubbed video and scroll driven, reversible 3D part movement.
- Visitors can rotate and separate 3D exhibits.
- The later city journey includes Yishang Street, Dongwu Intime twin towers, Taihu Longemont's International Circus as a clearly labeled part of the resort, and Nanxun Ancient Town. Visitors can switch real photographic viewpoints and compare two Nanxun river-lane angles.
- Must work on desktop and mobile and include a reduced motion alternative.
- All historic and craft claims must remain conservative and sourced.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two Feishu tutorials supplied by the user.
- A photograph of Nanxun Ancient Town from Wikimedia Commons, with source credit in the site.
- Eight additional Wikimedia Commons photographs, each with original author, work page and license in the site footer and `REFERENCE_RESEARCH.md`.
- 3D exhibits are interpretive digital models, not scans of museum objects. The tower uses an intentionally opened front bay to show the stone pagoda within.

## Product Principles

- The object leads the scene.
- One chapter teaches one idea.
- Scroll state is reversible.
- Distinguish interpretation from historical evidence.
